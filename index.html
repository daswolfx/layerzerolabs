THE PHYSICAL LAYER

The work starts with what you have.

A model. A broken part. A sketch. A strange idea that deserves to exist outside a screen.

Layer Zero Labs is a Georgia-based workshop for turning those starting points into practical FDM parts and prototypes. We work through the material, fit, build strategy, and finish with the intended use in mind.

  1. 01 / STARTING POINTBring the model, reference, or idea you already have.
  2. 02 / DECISIONSWork out geometry, material, fit, and finish.
  3. 03 / PHYSICAL RESULTMake the part, then evaluate its fit and finish.
